<p>When your theme and Judge.me both output product structured data (JSON-LD), Google Search Console can flag duplicate or conflicting Product schema and put your rich results at risk.</p>

<h2>Why it happens</h2>
<p>Most Shopify themes already print Product markup on every product page. Judge.me adds its own Product, AggregateRating and Review markup so that review stars can show in search results. When both are present, the page ends up with two competing descriptions of the same product, and Google may report errors or stop showing the stars.</p>

<h2>How I fix it</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Schema audit of your product pages</li>
  <li>Duplicate markup removed at the source</li>
  <li>Review stars and ratings kept intact in search results</li>
  <li>Google Search Console errors checked and cleared</li>
</ul>
<p>The key is removing the duplicate from the place that creates it, whether that is the theme or the app, rather than hiding it, so the fix survives theme and app updates.</p>

<h2>Check it yourself</h2>
<p>Open one of your product pages in Google's Rich Results Test and look at how many Product items it finds. More than one for the same product is the sign of duplicate schema. Search Console's Products report will show the same problem across the whole store.</p>
