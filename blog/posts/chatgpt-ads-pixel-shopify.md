<p>Ads inside ChatGPT are a new channel, and like every ad channel, they are only as useful as the data coming back from your store. That data comes from a small tracking snippet, the pixel. If it is missing or misplaced, you cannot tell which ads lead to orders.</p>

<h2>What the pixel does</h2>
<p>The pixel is a short script that loads on your storefront and reports what shoppers do. It has two parts:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>The base code</b>, which loads once on every page and identifies your store with your pixel ID.</li>
  <li><b>Event code</b>, which reports specific actions. The one that matters most is the purchase event, fired when an order is completed.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why the thank-you page matters</h2>
<p>The purchase event should fire only on the post-purchase thank-you page. Put it anywhere else, such as the cart or checkout, and it can report orders that never happened. Leave it off, and the platform sees clicks but no sales. Either way, the ads cannot learn which shoppers are worth reaching, and your reports show the wrong return.</p>
<p>Stores that offer free samples have one more thing to check. A sample order and a full order both land on the thank-you page, so decide up front whether both should be counted, and make sure the event fires for each as intended.</p>

<h2>How a correct setup helps a store</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Honest reporting.</b> Orders credited to the ads are real orders.</li>
  <li><b>Better optimization.</b> The ad platform can only improve targeting on conversions it actually sees.</li>
  <li><b>Smarter budgets.</b> You can compare this channel against your others with numbers you trust.</li>
  <li><b>No wasted spend on a broken setup.</b> Problems are found before the budget goes live, not after.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What a good installation looks like</h2>
<ul>
  <li>The base code added once, in the right place in the theme, and not duplicated by another app</li>
  <li>The purchase event added to the order thank-you page only</li>
  <li>Test orders placed, with the events checked in the pixel's debug mode</li>
  <li>Debug mode switched off once everything reports correctly</li>
</ul>
